Safety notes
Execution behavior
READ BEFORE ACTION
  • Pending LIMIT orders are not open positions; fill state is confirmed separately.
  • Cancel and close actions remain pending until Shark confirms the final order state.
  • LIVE position-level risk edits stay disabled until contract filters and split protection semantics are verified.
  • Unrealized P&L uses the last public price where fresh; it is not a mark-price or liquidation estimate.
